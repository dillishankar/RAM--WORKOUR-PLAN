7-DAY CYCLE · TECHNIQUE BEFORE LOAD

General rules

Warm-up8–10 min before every resistance workout
Compound exercises3 sets × 6–10 reps
Isolation exercises3 sets × 10–15 reps
Abs3 sets × 10–20 reps / 30–45 sec
Compound rest2–3 min
Isolation rest60–90 sec

Record your exercise, weight, reps and sets every workout. Increase weight only when you can perform the upper end of the rep range with good form.
